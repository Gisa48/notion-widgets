Notion Integration Quick Start

  1. Host these files on GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, or Vercel (100% free and permanent).
  2. In your Notion page, type /embed and press enter.
  3. Paste the direct URL to pomodoro/index.html or dopamine-menu/index.html.
  4. Drag the Notion handles to resize width and height to your taste.
